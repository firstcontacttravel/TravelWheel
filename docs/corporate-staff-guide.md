# TravelWheel Corporate: Staff Guide

A short guide for staff supporting corporate accounts. The build plan is in `corporate-flights-plan.md`.

## Who can do what

| Task | Who |
|---|---|
| Add, approve, reject, invite or suspend a company | CEO |
| Set company and default rates, the card fee and VAT | CEO |
| Assign an account manager | CEO |
| Book for a company | CEO, department heads and that company's account manager |
| Confirm or reject bank top-ups | Finance and the CEO |
| Credit a wallet, adjust a wallet or record a payout | Finance and the CEO |
| See invoices and download statements | Finance and the CEO |
| View a company's details | All staff |

## Bringing on a company

1. A request arrives through the public form, or you add the company under **Corporate > Companies**.
2. Check the CAC number.
3. Set rates with **Set rates**, or under **Corporate > Default Rates**. Without any, the company pays the public rate.
4. **Approve and invite**. The named contact gets a single-use link that lasts 7 days, and becomes the Company Admin.
5. Assign an account manager with **Set account manager**.

## Booking for a company

1. Open the company and choose **Book for this company**.
2. Search and book as usual. The bar along the bottom shows who you're booking for, and the company's rate and wallet apply.
3. Choose **Stop** on that bar when you're done.

The company sees these bookings as booked by TravelWheel. You're recorded on the booking, the wallet entry and the activity log. Enter the traveller's real contact email at checkout, because that's where the ticket goes.

## Money

- **Bank top-ups:** **Finance > Wallet Top-ups**. Confirm only once the money is in the bank, and enter the amount that actually arrived.
- **Card top-ups:** these confirm themselves. If a company has paid but the wallet hasn't moved, use **Check with SeerBit**.
- **Refunds:** on the flight booking, use **Credit wallet** with the amount after any penalties. The company sees your reason, and a credit note is issued. A refund from the supplier is a separate step.
- **Payouts:** send the money from the bank first, then use **Record payout** on the company.
- **Mistakes:** use **Adjust wallet** with a clear reason. Entries can't be edited, and the company sees the correction.

## When a wallet booking goes wrong

| What happened | What the system did | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The airline clearly refused | Returned the money and closed the booking | Nothing |
| A timeout or unclear answer | Kept the money and alerted operations | Check with the supplier. If the seat exists, ticket it. If not, use **Credit wallet** to return it all, which closes the booking |
| A ticket order failed on a hold | Kept the money and alerted operations | Retry the ticket, or use **Credit wallet** if the hold is abandoned |

Don't return money after an unclear answer until you've checked with the supplier, because the seat may exist.

## Suspending a company

**Suspend** signs everyone out at once. **If a company stays suspended for 30 days, its saved travellers and their passports are deleted automatically.** Reactivating before then cancels that. Wallet money, bookings and invoices are not affected.

## Passports

Saved passports are masked on every company page. Don't ask companies to email passport details: they can enter them on the Travellers page.

## If something looks wrong

The **System Health** page has a corporate wallet check. If it reports that a balance doesn't match the ledger, stop confirming top-ups for that company and tell IT straight away.
